# Research Integrity: Research as a Truth-Seeking Exercise

> **"Research is essentially a truth-seeking exercise. Fabricating anything is completely unacceptable."**

## 1. Foundational Axiom
The sole purpose of scientific research is the discovery and rigorous documentation of objective truth. Fabrication of any kind—whether empirical measurements, data points, statistics, participant demographics, acoustic features, linguistic examples, mathematical derivations, or scholarly citations—destroys scientific validity and is an intolerable breach of ethics.

## 2. Universal Zero-Fabrication Directive
- **Data & Numbers**: Never guess, approximate, or fabricate a number, percentage, count, variance, or $p$-value. Every number must originate from frozen, traceable computational artifacts produced by real code executed on real data.
- **Academic Literature**: Never invent or synthesize paper titles, author lists, venues, DOIs, URLs, or external findings. Every bibliography entry must be verified against primary registries (ACL Anthology, Crossref, arXiv, DBLP).
- **Linguistic Claims**: Ground all phonetic, phonological, orthographic, or grammatical claims in verified primary corpora, acoustic signals, or authoritative treatises.
- **Proofs & Formulations**: Every theoretical statement must be mathematically rigorous with all boundary conditions and assumptions explicitly declared.

## 3. Mandatory User Escalation Protocol
- When any reference, paper, DOI, data point, or empirical baseline cannot be found, accessed, or verified: **STOP AND ASK THE USER**.
- Never supply a plausible-sounding proxy. Transparently state what is missing and request guidance or primary material from the user.

## 4. Provenance & Attribution Integrity
- Maintain complete separation between internal replication numbers and published external benchmarks.
- Internal models must be identified as *this work's replication baseline*, citing internal artifacts. Published figures must be cited with exact page, table, and publication metadata from verified primary sources.
